FOUNDER

KM 3  /  Founder

Started by someone who dodged school sports day.

Millennia Rose was born and raised in Goa, loves art and fashion, and spent 26 years avoiding anything sporty. Then came running, in 2026. It changed Millennia's head, body and work, and brought a whole new set of ambitious, like-minded people along with it.

FORGED is what came next: a club built on the little details, where people feel welcome and look forward to moving. To be forged, for Millennia, means building something through hard work. It doesn't mean being ready first.

“Movement is a privilege, not a punishment.”
@millennia_rose_

Questions, answered

Do I need to be a runner to join FORGED?

No. FORGED is a social run club for every level: kids, teens, adults and elders. No experience and no pressure. Walk, jog or run at your own pace.

Is FORGED free to join?

Joining the FORGED WhatsApp group is free, and so are our runs: Chase the Sun and Run for the Mind were both free, with no registration. Just show up at the meeting point.

Where in Goa do FORGED runs happen?

All over Goa. So far we've run in Calangute, on the beach at Anjuna and on the Goa Velha Highway. Each event lists its meeting point.

When is the next FORGED run?

Jingle All The Way, FORGED’s Christmas run and rave, in December 2026. The date, time and meeting point land in the WhatsApp group first. Join the group.

How do I hear about new runs?

Join the FORGED WhatsApp group: routes, dates and registration land there first. You can also follow @the_forged.in on Instagram.

Who started FORGED?

Millennia Rose, born and raised in Goa, took up running in 2026 and built FORGED to make running social, welcoming and fun.
