KM 3  /  Founder

Started by someone who dodged school sports day.

Millennia Rose was born and raised in Goa, loves art and fashion, and spent 26 years avoiding anything sporty. Then came running, in 2026. It changed Millennia's head, body and work, and brought a whole new set of ambitious, like-minded people along with it.

FORGED is what came next: a club built on the little details, where people feel welcome and look forward to moving. To be forged, for Millennia, means building something through hard work. It doesn't mean being ready first.

“Movement is a privilege, not a punishment.”
@millennia_rose_
